About NYCAT

NYCAT is a locally operated practice application for the Specialized High Schools Admissions Test. It offers English and mathematics practice, records test responses and timing, and prepares results and explanations.

The application runs on the operator's Mac. This public information page does not host a practice test, database, backup archive, or sign-in service.

Google sign-in verifies a user's identity. A separate Google Drive connection lets the operator synchronize application data and maintain encrypted recovery backups in the operator's Google Drive.

NYCAT is an independent beta application. It is not an official examination service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NYC Public Schools, Pearson, or TestNav. Practice results do not guarantee an official score or admission.
